Het Archief — Writing Workshop and Activation

Impartido por Raúl Hilario Montes A Box Set With Three Corners — 2026

Through a series of writing exercises, participants will generate texts that will be deposited into the slots of the nine devices that make up the installation. Each of them already contains a previously inserted text. By participating, visitors become part of the work.

Until now, the texts inserted by the artist have remained undisclosed. In this way, for the remainder of the exhibition, both those who write and those who visit will share the same condition: that of a content that is present yet inaccessible, intimate and hidden.

The work is based on the idea that language is not fixed, but constantly transforms through its everyday use: in gestures, in movements, in errors and misunderstandings. In that intermediate space —between what is said and what is interpreted— new possibilities of meaning emerge. The devices operate as zones of transition between the public and the intimate, between interior and exterior.

The texts you write will remain as part of an ephemeral archive, in continuous displacement within the exhibition space, with the possibility of a future publication in which they may come to light.

Activation of the piece:

A/I/O/ D/L/R/ E/N/S/

Logo-rallye / Infiltrated phrase

Include a proverb / saying / common phrase

Evocation

Describe a space or object in detail without looking at it.

Epigenetic activation

List or introduce, repeatedly, words that resonate with you (from home, friends, frequent ones, favourites…).

Letter to a stranger

Write to a reader as if you already knew each other.

Ghost

Write about things that are not accessible to the eye at first glance.

Reinterpretation

Rewrite a poem by another author.

Mapping

Make a list by precisely enumerating places you usually pass through.

Ruis

Position yourself in front of a work in the space and write or draw the sound it transmits, or imagine its tone if it could speak.

Time machine

Pass the text through a time machine.
